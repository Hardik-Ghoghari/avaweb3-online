Third-Party Lending Program — Terms & Conditions

Third-Party Lending Program

The loan facility available through our platform is provided by independent third-party lenders. Our platform acts solely as a facilitator and intermediary between eligible borrowers and participating lenders. We do not directly provide the loan capital ourselves. Our role is to facilitate the lending process, provide the platform and related services, and assist with the administration of the loan arrangement.

By participating in the lending program, you acknowledge and agree that the applicable loan is funded by a third-party lender and is subject to the terms, conditions, requirements, and repayment obligations established for the loan.

Use of Your Assets as Collateral

As part of the lending arrangement, your eligible assets held through the platform may be used as collateral to secure the loan. The collateral requirement is intended to provide security to the third-party lender for the amount advanced to you.

While the loan remains outstanding, the assets designated as collateral may be subject to restrictions. These restrictions are intended to protect the lender's interests and ensure that sufficient collateral remains available until the loan has been fully repaid.

The specific amount of collateral required may depend on the size of the loan, the value of the assets provided as collateral, applicable lending requirements, and other conditions established by the participating lender.

Use of Loan Proceeds

Once the loan has been approved and made available, you may use the available loan proceeds together with your eligible assets for permitted activities on the platform.

Depending on the applicable program rules, the available funds may be used for:

  • Trading activities;
  • Participating in eligible contract plans;
  • Other investment-related activities made available through the platform.

The availability of loan proceeds does not mean that the borrowed funds become unrestricted personal funds. The loan remains outstanding until all applicable repayment obligations have been satisfied.

You are responsible for understanding the risks associated with using borrowed funds for trading or investment activities. Trading and investment activities may result in losses, and losses may affect your ability to satisfy your repayment obligations.

Withdrawal Restrictions

Because the loan is secured by your assets, withdrawals may be restricted while an outstanding loan balance remains unpaid.

You will not be permitted to withdraw the collateral or other restricted funds in a manner that would prevent the outstanding loan from being fully repaid. The purpose of this restriction is to ensure that the lender's secured position remains protected throughout the duration of the loan.

Before funds or assets become eligible for withdrawal, you may be required to repay the total outstanding loan amount, including any applicable accrued interest, fees, or other amounts owed under the applicable loan agreement.

This means that the loan must be treated as a financial obligation that remains separate from any trading profits or investment returns generated while using the borrowed funds.

Loan Repayment

The loan amount must be repaid through external deposits.

Funds generated through trading activities, contract-plan returns, or other internal balances may not be considered an eligible repayment source where the applicable loan terms require repayment through external deposits. Borrowers should therefore ensure that they have an appropriate external funding source available to meet their repayment obligations.

The borrower remains responsible for repaying the required amount regardless of whether trading or investment activities generate a profit or a loss.

Failure to make the required repayment may result in continued restrictions on the account and/or the application of other remedies available under the applicable loan agreement.

Annual Percentage Rate (APR)

The applicable loan carries an Annual Percentage Rate (APR) of 31%.

Interest is calculated and credited monthly. The interest is compounded, meaning that interest may be calculated not only on the original principal amount but also, where applicable under the loan terms, on previously accrued interest.

Because the interest compounds over time, the total amount owed may increase more quickly than it would under a simple-interest arrangement. Borrowers should carefully consider the total cost of borrowing and the length of time the loan remains outstanding before accepting the loan.

Monthly Compounding

The 31% APR represents the annualized rate applicable to the loan. Interest is credited on a monthly basis according to the applicable loan calculation methodology.

As an illustration, a loan that remains outstanding for an extended period may accumulate substantially more interest because of monthly compounding. The longer the loan remains unpaid, the greater the potential total repayment obligation.

The actual amount of interest charged will depend on the outstanding principal, the applicable calculation method, the timing of payments, and the duration of the loan.

Collateral and Loan Obligations

Using assets as collateral does not eliminate the borrower's obligation to repay the loan. The borrower remains responsible for satisfying the full repayment requirements established by the applicable loan agreement.

If the value of collateral changes, the lender or platform may have additional rights or requirements depending on the applicable agreement. Borrowers should understand that market fluctuations can affect the value of assets used as collateral.

Before entering into the lending program, borrowers should carefully review the collateral requirements, repayment conditions, interest calculation, withdrawal restrictions, and any applicable fees or other charges.

Important Risk Disclosure

Borrowing funds for trading or investment purposes involves significant financial risk. The use of leverage can increase both potential gains and potential losses. A decline in the value of assets may affect the borrower's financial position and ability to meet the loan obligations.

Borrowers should not assume that trading profits or contract-plan returns will be sufficient to repay the loan. Investment performance is not guaranteed, and past performance does not guarantee future results.

By participating in the lending program, the borrower acknowledges that they are responsible for understanding the risks associated with borrowing, trading, investing, and using assets as collateral.
